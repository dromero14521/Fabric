# Example Inputs for build_sales_funnel

Use one of the following input formats to design a complete sales funnel:

---

## Example 1: B2B SaaS

```
Business: CloudMetrics - Analytics platform for e-commerce
Model: B2B SaaS, subscription
Pricing: $99/mo (Starter), $299/mo (Growth), $799/mo (Scale), Enterprise (custom)

Target Audience:
- E-commerce store owners ($1M-50M revenue)
- Marketing managers at DTC brands
- Data-driven decision makers

Current State:
- 5,000 monthly website visitors
- 50 trial signups/month
- 10 paying customers/month (20% trial conversion)
- Average deal size: $350/month
- Sales cycle: 14-21 days

Marketing Channels:
- Organic SEO (40% of traffic)
- Google Ads (30%)
- LinkedIn content (20%)
- Referrals (10%)

Revenue Goals:
- Grow from $50k to $150k MRR in 12 months
- Need 300+ new customers
- Target CAC: under $500
- Target LTV:CAC ratio: 4:1

Primary Conversion Goal: Start free trial

Current Funnel Issues:
- Low trial-to-paid conversion (only 20%)
- Long sales cycle for enterprise
- No lead nurturing for visitors who don't sign up immediately
- Pricing page has high bounce rate
```

## Example 2: E-commerce / DTC Brand

```
Business: GlowSkin - Premium skincare brand
Model: B2C e-commerce, direct-to-consumer
Pricing: $35-120 per product, subscription boxes available ($89/month)

Target Audience:
- Women 28-45 years old
- Household income $75k+
- Interested in clean beauty, anti-aging
- Active on Instagram and YouTube

Current State:
- 25,000 monthly website visitors
- 2% conversion rate (500 orders/month)
- Average order value: $85
- Customer acquisition cost: $45
- 15% repeat purchase rate

Marketing Channels:
- Instagram/Facebook ads (50%)
- Influencer partnerships (25%)
- Organic social (15%)
- Email (10%)

Revenue Goals:
- Increase monthly revenue from $42k to $100k
- Improve conversion rate to 3.5%
- Increase repeat purchase rate to 30%
- Reduce CAC to $35

Primary Conversion Goal: First purchase

Current Funnel Issues:
- High cart abandonment (75%)
- Low email capture on site (only 1.5%)
- No post-purchase nurture sequence
- Subscription program underperforming
```

## Example 4: Online Course / Info Product

```
Business: LeadershipLab - Executive coaching and courses
Model: Info product + coaching
Pricing:
- Self-paced course: $497
- Group coaching program: $2,997 (12 weeks)
- 1:1 coaching: $1,500/month

Target Audience:
- Mid-level managers wanting promotion
- New executives (first 2 years in role)
- Entrepreneurs scaling teams
- Age 30-50, $100k+ income

Current State:
- Email list: 3,000 subscribers
- 500 monthly website visitors
- Course sales: 10/month ($5k/month)
- Coaching clients: 3 active
- Webinar attendance: 50 people/month, 5% buy

Marketing Channels:
- Organic LinkedIn (60%)
- Email list (30%)
- Podcast guest appearances (10%)

Revenue Goals:
- Grow from $10k to $50k/month
- Launch group coaching program
- Build email list to 15,000
- Automate course sales

Primary Conversion Goal: Register for free webinar → Buy course

Current Funnel Issues:
- Webinar conversion rate is low (5%)
- No tripwire or low-ticket offer
- Email open rates declining (now 18%)
- No evergreen funnel (everything is live launches)
```

## Example 5: Local Service Business

```
Business: SparkClean - Residential cleaning service
Model: Local service, recurring
Pricing:
- One-time deep clean: $200-400
- Weekly service: $120-200/visit
- Bi-weekly service: $140-220/visit
- Monthly service: $160-250/visit

Target Audience:
- Dual-income households
- Busy professionals
- Families with children
- Homeowners (not renters)
- Service area: Austin, TX metro

Current State:
- 800 monthly website visitors
- 20 quote requests/month
- Close rate: 50% (10 new customers/month)
- Average customer value: $3,600/year (bi-weekly)
- Customer retention: 18 months average

Marketing Channels:
- Google search (50%)
- Nextdoor and local Facebook groups (30%)
- Referrals (20%)

Revenue Goals:
- Add 30 new recurring customers/month
- Reduce cost per lead from $80 to $40
- Increase close rate to 70%
- Build referral program

Primary Conversion Goal: Request a quote

Current Funnel Issues:
- Website doesn't build trust (no reviews shown)
- No follow-up on unconverted quotes
- Price shoppers dominate inquiries
- No differentiation from competitors
```

## Example 6: Marketplace / Platform

```
Business: FreelanceHub - Freelance marketplace for designers
Model: Two-sided marketplace, transaction fees
Pricing:
- Freelancers: 10% fee on earnings
- Clients: Free to post, 3% service fee
- Premium listing: $29/month for freelancers

Target Audiences:
- FREELANCERS: Graphic designers, UI/UX designers, illustrators
- CLIENTS: Startups, marketing agencies, small businesses

Current State:
- 10,000 monthly visitors
- 200 freelancer signups/month
- 50 client signups/month
- 20 completed projects/month
- Average project value: $1,500

Marketing Channels:
- Organic SEO (45%)
- Social media (30%)
- Partnerships with design tools (15%)
- Paid ads (10%)

Revenue Goals:
- 100 completed projects/month
- 500 active freelancers
- Improve client-to-project rate from 40% to 60%
- Reduce freelancer churn

Primary Conversion Goals:
- Freelancers: Complete profile + portfolio
- Clients: Post first project

Current Funnel Issues:
- Chicken-and-egg problem (need both sides)
- Low freelancer profile completion (only 30%)
- Clients post project but don't hire (trust issues)
- No nurturing for inactive users
```
