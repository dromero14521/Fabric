# Generate Lead Magnet

Create high-converting lead magnets (ebooks, checklists, templates, calculators) that attract qualified leads.

## What You'll Get
- 5-7 lead magnet concept ideas with detailed outlines
- Landing page copy (headline, benefits, CTA)
- Email delivery sequence (5 emails)
- Promotion strategy (organic + paid channels)
- Production checklist and timeline
- Success metrics and A/B testing framework

## What to Provide
- Target audience (role, industry, company size, pain points)
- Buyer journey stage (awareness, consideration, decision)
- Your business offering and unique value prop
- Competitors' lead magnets (what exists already)
- Resources available (time, budget, design capability)
- Desired outcome (lead volume vs. quality balance)

## Example Input
```
Target Audience: B2B SaaS marketing directors at companies with 50-500 employees
Pain Points:
- Struggle to generate qualified leads (not just volume)
- Limited budget for paid ads
- Content marketing takes too long to produce

Buyer Stage: Consideration (they know they need better lead gen, researching solutions)

Our Offering: Marketing automation platform that helps B2B companies build lead gen systems

Competitors' Lead Magnets:
- "10 Lead Gen Tips" (generic listicle)
- "Ultimate Marketing Guide" (300-page ebook, too long)

Resources: Can dedicate 20 hours to creation, have designer available, budget $500 for promotion

Goal: Generate 200+ qualified leads (directors+ at target companies), 30%+ landing page conversion
```

## Use Cases
- Launch new product/service and need leads
- Build email list from scratch
- Attract specific buyer personas
- Generate sales pipeline
- Establish thought leadership
