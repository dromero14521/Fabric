# Qualify Leads

Comprehensive lead qualification using BANT, MEDDIC, and CHAMP frameworks with scoring and prioritization.

## What You'll Get
- Lead score (0-100) with tier classification (Hot/Warm/Cold/Disqualified)
- ICP fit analysis with detailed breakdown
- Buying intent assessment
- BANT qualification (Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline)
- MEDDIC analysis (for enterprise deals)
- Red flags and risk assessment
- Specific next actions and recommended owner
- Discovery question templates

## What to Provide
- Company information (name, size, industry, revenue)
- Contact details (name, title, role, seniority)
- Lead source (inbound, outbound, referral, event)
- Engagement signals (downloads, demo requests, pricing inquiries)
- Any budget, timeline, or urgency indicators
- Your ICP criteria (ideal company size, industries, budget range)

## Example Input
```
Lead Info:
Company: Acme Software Inc.
Size: 250 employees
Industry: B2B SaaS
Revenue: $20M-$50M ARR
Location: San Francisco, CA
Tech Stack: Salesforce, HubSpot, AWS

Contact: Sarah Chen, VP of Sales
Email: sarah.chen@acmesoftware.com
Seniority: C-Level equivalent

Source: Downloaded our "Sales Automation ROI Calculator"
Engagement: Visited pricing page 3 times, opened last 2 emails

Timeline Signal: Mentioned "need solution before Q4" in form
Budget Signal: None explicitly mentioned yet

Our ICP:
- B2B SaaS companies
- 100-1000 employees
- $10M-$100M revenue
- Sales or marketing tech stack
- Active in US market
```

## Use Cases
- Prioritize incoming leads for sales team
- Decide which prospects to pursue vs nurture
- Calculate expected close probability
- Optimize sales resource allocation
- Reduce time wasted on poor-fit prospects
- Improve sales forecast accuracy
