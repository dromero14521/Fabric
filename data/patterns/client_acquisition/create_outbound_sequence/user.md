# Create Outbound Sequence

Generate personalized B2B/B2C outbound sales sequences with multi-channel touchpoints.

## What You'll Get
- 7-email sequence with A/B test variations
- LinkedIn connection messages
- Cold call scripts and voicemail templates
- Deliverability optimization checklist
- CRM setup instructions

## What to Provide
- Target industry and ideal customer profile
- Your value proposition
- Top 3 pain points you solve
- Social proof (case studies, testimonials, metrics)
- Desired call-to-action (demo, call, trial, etc.)

## Example Input
```
Target industry: B2B SaaS companies (50-500 employees)
Value proposition: AI-powered customer support automation that reduces response time by 80%
Pain points:
1. Long customer wait times (avg 4 hours)
2. High support costs ($50/ticket)
3. Inconsistent response quality
Social proof: Reduced support costs by 60% for TechCorp (saved $200k/year)
CTA: Book 15-minute demo
```
