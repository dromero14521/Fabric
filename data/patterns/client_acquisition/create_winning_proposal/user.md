# Create Winning Proposal

High-converting client proposals with quantified ROI and flexible pricing.

## What You'll Get
- Executive summary with business value
- Situation analysis (current vs. future state)
- Phased solution approach
- Good/Better/Best pricing packages
- Timeline with milestones
- Case studies and testimonials
- Risk mitigation and guarantees

## What to Provide
- Client name, industry, company size
- Project type (website, automation, consulting, etc.)
- Client's primary business problem/goal
- Current pain points
- Desired outcomes (specific and measurable)
- Budget range (if known)
- Timeline expectations

## Example Input
```
Client: Acme Corp (e-commerce, 50 employees)
Project: Custom Shopify app for inventory automation
Pain points:
- Manual inventory updates take 10 hours/week ($500/week labor cost)
- Frequent stock-outs causing $5k/month in lost sales
- No real-time sync between POS and online store
Desired outcomes:
- Automate inventory sync (save 8 hours/week)
- Reduce stock-outs by 90%
- Real-time visibility across all channels
Budget: $15,000-$25,000
Timeline: Need solution live in 10 weeks (peak season approaching)
Decision maker: Director of Operations (tech-savvy, budget-conscious)
```
