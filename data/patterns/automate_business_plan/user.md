# Example Inputs for automate_business_plan

Use one of the following input formats to generate an executable automation blueprint:

---

## Example 1: Digital Marketing Agency

```
Business Plan Summary:

Business: GrowthSpark Digital - Full-service digital marketing agency
Target Clients: B2B SaaS companies ($1M-20M ARR)
Services:
- SEO and content marketing
- Paid advertising (Google, LinkedIn, Meta)
- Marketing automation and CRM setup
- Conversion rate optimization

Operational Pillars:
1. Lead Generation: Inbound content + cold outreach
2. Sales: Discovery calls → proposals → close
3. Service Delivery: Client onboarding → monthly retainers
4. Client Success: Reporting, QBRs, renewals

Team: 2 founders, 3 contractors
Revenue Goal: $50k MRR in 12 months

Current Pain Points:
- Manually tracking leads in spreadsheets
- No automated follow-up sequences
- Reports are created manually each month (8 hours per client)
- No systemized onboarding process

Automation Priorities:
- Lead capture and CRM automation
- Email nurture sequences
- Client reporting automation
- Proposal generation

Tech Stack Available:
- HubSpot (free tier)
- Slack
- Google Workspace
- Notion
```

## Example 2: E-commerce Business

```
Business Plan Summary:

Business: PetBox Co - Subscription box for dog owners
Model: D2C e-commerce with subscription focus
Products:
- Monthly surprise box ($39/month)
- Quarterly themed box ($99/quarter)
- One-time gift boxes ($49-89)

Operational Pillars:
1. Customer Acquisition: Facebook/Instagram ads → Landing pages
2. Order Fulfillment: Inventory → Packing → Shipping
3. Customer Retention: Email marketing, loyalty program
4. Customer Support: Returns, inquiries, subscription management

Team: 1 founder, 1 VA, 3PL for fulfillment
Revenue Goal: 1,000 active subscribers ($40k MRR)

Current Pain Points:
- Manually importing orders to 3PL
- No automated win-back for churned subscribers
- Customer support is all manual email
- Inventory tracking is in spreadsheets
- Social media posting is inconsistent

Automation Priorities:
- Order → fulfillment automation
- Subscription lifecycle emails (welcome, renewal, win-back)
- Inventory low-stock alerts
- Social media scheduling
- Customer support chatbot for FAQs

Tech Stack Available:
- Shopify
- Klaviyo
- ShipStation
- Zapier (paid plan)
- Gorgias (considering)
```

## Example 3: SaaS Product

```
Business Plan Summary:

Business: FormFlow - No-code form builder for marketers
Model: SaaS, freemium
Pricing: Free (3 forms), Pro $29/mo, Team $79/mo

Operational Pillars:
1. Product: Feature development, bug fixes, infrastructure
2. Growth: Content marketing, SEO, product-led growth
3. Sales: Self-service + sales-assisted for Team plans
4. Customer Success: Onboarding, support, expansion

Team: 2 co-founders (1 technical, 1 business)
Revenue Goal: $20k MRR, 700 paying customers

Current Pain Points:
- No automated onboarding emails
- Trial users don't convert (only 5%)
- No usage-based triggers for upsells
- Support tickets handled manually
- Content publishing is manual

Automation Priorities:
- Trial onboarding email sequence
- Usage tracking → upsell triggers
- Support ticket routing and auto-responses
- Content calendar and publishing automation
- Churn risk detection and alerts

Tech Stack Available:
- AWS infrastructure
- Segment for analytics
- Intercom for support
- ConvertKit for email
- Linear for project management
```

## Example 4: Consulting / Coaching Business

```
Business Plan Summary:

Business: ScaleUp Coaching - Business coaching for agency owners
Model: High-ticket coaching + group program
Pricing:
- 1:1 Coaching: $3,000/month
- Group Mastermind: $12,000/year
- Workshop: $497 one-time

Operational Pillars:
1. Lead Generation: LinkedIn content, podcast, speaking
2. Sales: DMs → Discovery call → Enrollment call
3. Delivery: Coaching calls, resources, community
4. Retention: Renewals, referrals, testimonials

Team: 1 coach, 1 VA
Revenue Goal: $30k/month (8 1:1 clients + 50 group members)

Current Pain Points:
- LinkedIn engagement is manual (2 hours/day)
- No system for tracking leads and follow-ups
- Calendar scheduling requires back-and-forth
- Client resources scattered across Google Drive
- No automated testimonial collection

Automation Priorities:
- LinkedIn engagement automation (comments, DMs)
- Lead tracking and pipeline management
- Automated calendar booking with reminders
- Client portal with organized resources
- Automated review/testimonial requests
- Content repurposing (podcast → clips → posts)

Tech Stack Available:
- Notion
- Calendly
- Zoom
- Circle (community)
- Riverside (podcast)
```

## Example 5: Local Service Business

```
Business Plan Summary:

Business: ProPlumb Solutions - Residential plumbing company
Model: Local service, emergency + scheduled
Pricing:
- Service call: $89 dispatch fee
- Hourly rate: $120-180/hour
- Common jobs: $150-2,500

Operational Pillars:
1. Lead Generation: Google Ads, SEO, referrals
2. Dispatch: Answering calls → scheduling → dispatching techs
3. Service Delivery: On-site work → invoicing → payment
4. Follow-up: Reviews, maintenance reminders, referral asks

Team: Owner/operator, 2 technicians, 1 office manager
Revenue Goal: $80k/month, 200+ jobs

Current Pain Points:
- Missed calls during busy hours = lost leads
- Scheduling is manual (whiteboard + phone)
- Invoices sent manually, often late
- No automated review requests
- No follow-up for unsold estimates
- Techs don't have mobile access to job details

Automation Priorities:
- 24/7 call answering or callback system
- Online booking and scheduling
- Automated dispatch notifications to techs
- Instant invoicing and payment collection
- Automated review requests (SMS)
- Follow-up on unsold estimates
- Maintenance reminder emails

Tech Stack Available:
- QuickBooks
- Google Workspace
- Basic website (WordPress)
- Considering: ServiceTitan, Jobber, or Housecall Pro
```
