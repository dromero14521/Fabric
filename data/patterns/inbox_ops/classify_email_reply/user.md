# Example Inputs for classify_email_reply

Use one of the following input formats to classify email replies:

---

## Example 1: Clearly Interested

```
From: mike@abcplumbing.com
Subject: Re: Quick question about ABC Plumbing's website

Yeah this is something I've been thinking about. What does it cost? We're pretty slammed right now but I'd like to learn more.

Mike
```

---

## Example 2: Question (Intent Unclear)

```
From: sarah@sunshinedentalcare.com
Subject: RE: Online booking for Sunshine Dental

How does this work exactly? Do patients book directly or does it go through our front desk first?

Dr. Sarah Smith
```

---

## Example 3: Timing Objection

```
From: tom@cozyhomeremodeling.com
Subject: Re: Quick question about your website

Thanks for reaching out. We're in the middle of a big project right now and can't really think about this until spring. Check back in March?

Tom
```

---

## Example 4: Clear Rejection

```
From: info@johnsonlaw.com
Subject: RE: 24/7 intake for Johnson Law

We're not interested. Please remove us from your list.
```

---

## Example 5: Auto-Reply / Out of Office

```
From: amanda@peakperformance.com
Subject: Automatic Reply: Out of Office

Thank you for your email. I am currently out of the office until January 15th with limited access to email. For urgent matters, please contact our front desk at 555-123-4567.

Best,
Amanda Chen
```

---

## Example 6: Price Objection

```
From: carlos@greenthumblandscaping.com
Subject: Re: Quote request form for Green Thumb

I looked at your website. How much does this cost? We're a small operation and don't have a big budget for tech stuff.

Carlos
```

---

## Example 7: Authority Objection

```
From: jennifer@eliteautorepair.com
Subject: RE: Online scheduling

This sounds interesting but I'd need to run it by Marcus (the owner). Can you send me some info I can share with him?

Jennifer
Shop Manager
```

---

## Example 8: Hot Lead (Urgent)

```
From: david@davidjohnsonlaw.com
Subject: Re: Your email about intake

Yes let's talk. Can you call me today? My direct line is 312-555-9999. I'm free after 2pm.

David Johnson
```

---

## Example 9: Competitor/Research

```
From: alex@competitoragency.com
Subject: Re: AI chatbot services

Interesting approach. What platform do you use for the chatbot? We're evaluating different solutions for our clients.

Alex
Digital Agency
```

---

## Example 10: Confused/Wrong Person

```
From: bob@bobshvac.com
Subject: RE: Website services

I think you have the wrong person? I don't remember asking about this. What is this regarding?

Bob
```

---

## Example 11: Spam/Irrelevant

```
From: marketing@randomcompany.com
Subject: RE: Partnership Opportunity

Thanks for your interest! Check out our amazing SEO services at www.randomcompany.com. We can get you to page 1 of Google in 30 days GUARANTEED!!!

Click here for a free consultation!
```

---

## Example 12: Positive but Vague

```
From: owner@localbusiness.com
Subject: Re: Your email

Sounds good. Let me know more.
```

---

## Example 13: Multi-Part Response

```
From: marcus@eliteauto.com
Subject: Re: Online booking system

A few questions:

1. What's the monthly cost?
2. Does it integrate with our current software (we use Shop-Ware)?
3. How long does setup take?
4. Can customers book specific services or just general appointments?

I've been meaning to add online booking for a while. If the price is right and it works with our system, I'm interested.

Marcus
Owner, Elite Auto Repair
```

---

## Example 14: Referral/Redirect

```
From: frontdesk@medicaloffice.com
Subject: RE: Patient booking system

Thanks for reaching out. I'm forwarding this to Dr. Patel who handles these decisions. He should get back to you.

Rachel
Front Office
```

---

## Example 15: With Original Email Thread

```
From: lisa@cleaningsvc.com
Subject: Re: Quick question about CleanPro Services

Original Email:
> Hi Lisa,
>
> I noticed CleanPro doesn't have online booking on your website. When customers search "cleaning service near me" at 9pm, they can't schedule with you - they book with whoever makes it easy.
>
> I help cleaning companies add 24/7 booking. One of my clients got 15 new recurring customers in the first month.
>
> Worth a quick call?
>
> Alex

Reply:
How does the booking work? Does the customer pick a specific time or just request a callback?

Lisa
CleanPro Services
```

---

## Quick Input Template

```
From: [sender email]
Subject: [subject line]
Date: [if available]

[Paste the full email reply here]

---
Original Outreach (if helpful for context):
[Your original email that they're replying to]
```
