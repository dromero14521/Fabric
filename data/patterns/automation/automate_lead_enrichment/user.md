# Automate Lead Enrichment

Automated lead data enrichment with company info, email verification, and lead scoring.

## What You'll Get
- Complete automation workflow (Zapier/n8n/custom)
- API integration setup (Clearbit, Hunter.io, etc.)
- Lead scoring algorithm (ICP fit)
- CRM integration and routing rules
- Cost analysis and ROI calculation
- Error handling and edge cases

## What to Provide
- Lead data source (web form, CSV, API)
- CRM system (HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, etc.)
- Enrichment needs (company data, tech stack, email verification)
- Budget constraints (free tools only or paid APIs)
- Lead volume (per month)
- ICP criteria (target company size, industries, technologies)
- Sales workflow (how leads should be routed)

## Example Input
```
Lead source: Website contact form (Typeform)
CRM: HubSpot
Enrichment needed:
- Company size and revenue
- Industry classification
- Technologies used (especially Salesforce, HubSpot, Shopify)
- Email verification (catch typos and fake emails)
- LinkedIn profile discovery
Budget: Up to $250/month for APIs
Volume: ~500 leads/month
ICP: B2B SaaS companies, 50-500 employees, using Salesforce or HubSpot, in North America
Sales workflow:
- Score >80: Auto-assign to senior AE + Slack notification
- Score 60-79: Assign to SDR + add to outbound sequence
- Score <60: Add to nurture campaign
```
