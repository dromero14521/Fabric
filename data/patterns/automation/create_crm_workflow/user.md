# Create CRM Workflow

Design intelligent CRM automation workflows for lead routing, task management, email sequences, and sales pipeline optimization.

## What You'll Get
- Complete CRM workflow design for your sales process
- Lead routing rules (round-robin, territory, skill-based)
- Automated task creation by stage
- Email sequence triggers and templates
- Lead scoring automation (demographic + behavioral)
- Slack/email notifications and alerts
- Reporting dashboards for reps and managers
- Data hygiene workflows (duplicates, archiving)
- Integration setup guides (email, calendar, marketing)
- Implementation roadmap (6-week plan)

## What to Provide
- CRM platform (HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho, other)
- Team structure (SDRs, AEs, CSMs, territories)
- Sales process stages (your current workflow)
- Current pain points (slow response, missing follow-ups, data entry)
- Lead sources (web forms, outbound, referrals, events)
- Desired automations (what should be automated?)
- Integration needs (email, marketing tools, support)

## Example Input
```
CRM: HubSpot
Team: 3 SDRs, 5 AEs, 2 CSMs
Regions: 3 SDRs/AEs on West Coast, 2 AEs on East Coast

Sales Stages:
1. New Lead → 2. Qualified → 3. Demo → 4. Proposal → 5. Closed-Won/Lost

Current Pain Points:
- New leads sit unassigned for hours (need instant routing)
- Reps forget to follow up after demos (need automated reminders)
- No visibility into why deals are lost (need loss reason tracking)
- Too much manual data entry (need activity logging)

Lead Sources:
- Website forms (50/month)
- Outbound prospecting (200/month)
- Referrals (20/month)
- Trade shows (30/month)

Desired Automations:
- Auto-assign leads within 5 minutes
- Create follow-up tasks automatically
- Send post-demo email sequences
- Alert team on hot leads (score >80)
- Notify manager when large deals are lost

Integrations:
- Gmail (log emails automatically)
- Google Calendar (sync meetings)
- Slack (notifications)
```

## Use Cases
- Setting up CRM for the first time
- Sales team spending too much time on admin work
- Leads falling through the cracks (slow response times)
- No visibility into pipeline health
- Inconsistent follow-up processes
- Want to scale sales team efficiently
