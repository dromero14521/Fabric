# Optimize Pricing Strategy

Design value-based pricing strategies with psychological pricing, competitive analysis, and tier optimization.

## What You'll Get
- Recommended pricing model (flat rate, tiered, usage-based, hybrid)
- Good/Better/Best tier structure with optimal price points
- Competitive pricing analysis and market positioning
- Value-to-price ratio calculations
- Pricing psychology applications (anchoring, charm pricing, framing)
- Annual vs. monthly pricing recommendations
- Discount and negotiation guidelines
- Pricing page design recommendations
- A/B testing framework
- Financial impact projections

## What to Provide
- Current offering (product/service, features, benefits)
- Target customers (B2B/B2C, segments, buyer personas)
- Current pricing (if any) and performance
- Cost structure (COGS, delivery costs, fixed costs)
- Competitors and their pricing
- Value delivered (ROI, time saved, revenue impact)
- Business goals (revenue, market share, profit margin)

## Example Input
```
Offering: B2B SaaS project management tool
Features: Task management, team collaboration, time tracking, reporting, integrations

Target Customers:
- SMBs (10-50 employees)
- Tech companies and agencies
- Budget-conscious but value quality

Current Pricing: $49/month flat rate (not converting well)

Costs:
- COGS: $8/customer/month (hosting, APIs)
- Fixed costs: $20k/month (team, overhead)

Competitors:
- Asana: $10.99/user/month
- Monday.com: $8-16/user/month
- Basecamp: $99/month unlimited users

Value Delivered:
- Saves 10 hours/week in team coordination ($500/week value)
- Reduces missed deadlines by 60%
- Improves project visibility

Goals: Increase ARPU from $49 to $100-150, maintain >30% conversion rate
```

## Use Cases
- Launching new product and need pricing strategy
- Current pricing isn't converting well
- Want to increase revenue without losing customers
- Need tiered pricing structure
- Preparing for fundraising (need pricing justification)
