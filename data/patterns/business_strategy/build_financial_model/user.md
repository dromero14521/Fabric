# Build Financial Model

Create investor-grade 3-year financial projections with unit economics.

## What You'll Get
- 36-month P&L and cash flow statements
- Unit economics (CAC, LTV, LTV:CAC ratio, burn rate, runway)
- Best/Likely/Worst case scenarios
- Break-even analysis
- Sensitivity analysis
- Investor-ready summary

## What to Provide
- Business model type (SaaS, agency, consulting, etc.)
- Pricing structure
- Current state (pre-revenue, early traction, etc.)
- Cost of goods sold / delivery costs
- Fixed operating expenses
- Growth assumptions (CAC, churn, acquisition rate)

## Example Input
```
Business model: B2B SaaS
Pricing: $99/mo (Pro), $299/mo (Enterprise)
Current state: Pre-revenue, launching in 30 days
Target: $1M ARR by Month 24
COGS: $15/customer/month (hosting, APIs, support)
Team: 2 founders ($0 salary initially), hire engineer Month 6 ($120k)
Growth: Targeting 50 customers by Month 6, 500 by Month 12
CAC: Estimate $500 initially, optimizing to $200 by Month 12
Churn: Assuming 5% monthly (industry avg 7%)
```
