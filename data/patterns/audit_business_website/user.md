## Example 5: E-commerce/Retail Business

```
Business: Cozy Candle Co.
Type: Small e-commerce candle business (also has local retail)
Location: Portland, OR

Website Content Summary:
- Homepage shows product grid immediately
- No clear headline or value proposition
- Products listed: Lavender Dreams ($24), Ocean Breeze ($24), Vanilla Bean ($24), etc.
- "Add to Cart" buttons on each product
- About page mentions "hand-poured with love since 2020"
- No customer reviews displayed on site
- Checkout requires account creation
- Shipping info buried in FAQ
- No local pickup option shown (but they offer it)
- Instagram link in footer (2,400 followers)
- No email signup/newsletter
- Site loads slowly on mobile
- Copyright says 2021

Questions:
- Are we leaving money on the table?
- Why do people abandon carts?
- Should we focus on local or online?
```
