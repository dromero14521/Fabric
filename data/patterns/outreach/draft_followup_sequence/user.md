# Example Inputs for draft_followup_sequence

Use one of the following input formats to generate a follow-up email sequence:

---

## Example 1: Basic Context

```
Original Email Sent: February 5, 2026
Business: Mike's Plumbing
Owner: Mike Johnson
Location: Seattle, WA
Gap: No online booking
My Service: AI chatbot + booking system

Original email opened but no reply.
```

---

## Example 2: Full Original Email + Context

```
Original Email:

Subject: Quick question about ABC Plumbing's website

Hi Mike,

I was looking for a plumber in Seattle and found ABC Plumbing - great reviews on Google.

I noticed you don't have online booking on your site. That means when someone's pipe bursts at 10pm, they can't schedule with you - they'll call whoever shows up first.

I help plumbers add 24/7 booking and lead capture. One of my clients got 12 new jobs in the first month just from after-hours requests.

Worth a quick call?

Alex

---

Sent: Monday, February 5, 10am
Status: Opened twice, no reply
Business Type: Residential plumbing
Business Size: 3 technicians
Proof Points Available:
- Seattle plumber client got 12 jobs in first month
- Average ROI is 5x for service businesses
- Setup takes 48 hours

Create 5-email follow-up sequence.
```

2. Helps her sell internally to Dr. Smith
